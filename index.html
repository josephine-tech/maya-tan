Meet Maya

Maya Tan is an independent growth consultant with over a decade of experience helping businesses scale across Southeast Asia. She has advised venture-backed startups, high-growth e-commerce brands, SaaS companies, and established businesses on growth strategy, market expansion, customer acquisition, and operational scaling.

Her approach combines strategic thinking with practical execution, helping businesses move from ambitious plans to measurable outcomes.
